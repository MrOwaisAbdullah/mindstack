{% comment %} Customer impersonation banner. Rendered above the body of every page in `base.html` when `ImpersonationBannerMiddleware` (apps/accounts/middleware.py) stamps `request.impersonation_active = True` from the `session["impersonating"]` blob. Why the loud red: this banner is the operator's only persistent reminder that any state-changing click on the page will be attributed to the TARGET user, not them. A subtle yellow strip is too easy to miss after a few minutes of debugging. The End-impersonation link goes to the `account_impersonate_end` GET view (registered in apps/accounts/urls.py), which pops the session blob, rotates the CSRF token, restores the operator's identity, and redirects back to /admin/users//. Idempotent — repeated clicks land harmlessly on /. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %} {% if request.impersonation_active %}
